Where to Stay

Palm Beach's finest addresses

Palm Beach has a short list of truly great hotels — and each has its own distinct personality. The choice here shapes everything else about your trip.

Iconic · The Palm Beach Institution
The Breakers Palm Beach
The grande dame of Florida luxury — a 538-room oceanfront palazzo in business since 1896. Italian Renaissance architecture, multiple pools, a private beach, and more restaurants than you can visit in a week.
OceanfrontPrivate Beach3 Pools10 RestaurantsSpa
✦Request an ocean-facing room in the main building — the sunrise views justify the upgrade. The Seafood Bar is the best lunch on property; don't skip it.
Boutique · Most Intimate
Four Seasons Resort Palm Beach
More intimate than The Breakers — 210 rooms, Michelin-starred dining from chef Mauro Colagreco, and a beach experience that feels genuinely private. Pastel-toned rooms with balconies, superb service, and the best beach setup in town.
Michelin-Starred DiningBeachfrontIntimate ScaleFora Preferred
✦The Four Seasons is where I send couples and honeymooners. It has the warmth and scale that The Breakers, with its convention-center size, can't quite replicate.
Iconic · Most Palm Beach
The Colony Hotel Palm Beach
Bubblegum pink, Worth Avenue adjacent, and dripping with old Palm Beach charm. The Colony's pool bar and Sunday brunch are two of the great social rituals of the season. See and be seen — this is how Palm Beach is supposed to feel.
Worth Ave AdjacentIconic PoolSunday BrunchSocial Scene
✦Book a room that overlooks the pool. The Colony's social energy — families, divorcées, socialites, everyone in Lilly — is half the reason to come to Palm Beach.
Boutique · Hidden Gem
The Brazilian Court Hotel
A 1926 courtyard hotel with a Mediterranean soul — and home to Café Boulud Palm Beach, Daniel Boulud's outpost that has become one of the best restaurants in Florida. Understated elegance in the heart of town.

Things to Do

Palm Beach, properly done

Palm Beach rewards those who know where to look. Beyond the beach and the shopping, there's a cultural life and a social rhythm that takes a few trips to fully understand.

01
Worth Avenue — A Morning Well Spent
Rodeo Drive's East Coast rival — four blocks of designer boutiques, art galleries, and hidden courtyards called "vias" that wind behind the storefronts. Walk the entire length, duck into every via, and end at Sant Ambroeus for lunch. Don't rush this.
🛍️ Morning Experience
02
The Norton Museum of Art
One of the finest regional art museums in America — Matisse, Picasso, Hopper, Warhol, and a stunning collection of Chinese art housed in a beautifully renovated building. Free on Saturday mornings. Essential Palm Beach culture.
🎨 Cultural Experience
03
The Lake Trail at Sunrise
A paved trail running the length of Palm Beach island along Lake Worth — past the mansions, the private docks, and the most extraordinary real estate in Florida. Walk or rent a bike. Best before 8am when the light is golden and the street is empty.
🌅 Morning Ritual
04
Sunday Brunch at The Colony
The Colony's Sunday poolside brunch is one of the great social rituals of the Palm Beach season — champagne, beautiful food, beautiful people, and that unmistakable pink backdrop. Reserve weeks ahead; it fills completely during season.
🥂 Social Experience
05
Deep-Sea Fishing Charter
The Gulf Stream runs unusually close to shore at Palm Beach — some of the best bluewater fishing on the East Coast is just minutes offshore. Mahi, wahoo, sailfish, and marlin are all in season at different times. Half-day charters depart from Lake Worth Inlet.
🎣 Half-Day Charter
06
Flagler Museum (Whitehall)
Henry Flagler's 1902 Beaux-Arts mansion — the man who literally built Florida's East Coast — is now a museum of extraordinary opulence. Gold leaf, marble, and a private railway car in the backyard. One of the finest Gilded Age homes in America.
🏛️ Historic Experience
Getting There

Arriving in Palm Beach

Palm Beach is served by three airports at varying distances — and the right choice depends on where you're coming from and where you're staying.

✈️
Palm Beach International (PBI)
The closest airport — 10 minutes from the island. Served by American, Delta, JetBlue, and Southwest with direct routes from major Eastern hubs. The easy answer for most trips.
Always check PBI first — the proximity to the island saves 45+ minutes versus FLL.
✈️
Fort Lauderdale (FLL)
About 70 miles south and served by every major carrier including budget airlines. Worth considering for better flight options or lower fares — budget 60–75 minutes for the drive north to Palm Beach.
Spirit and Frontier fly into FLL; if the fare gap is significant, the extra drive time is manageable.
🚗
Getting Around On-Island
Palm Beach island is only 14 miles long and a mile wide — a car is useful but not always necessary for a hotel-based stay. The Breakers and Four Seasons both offer bikes. Worth Avenue is walkable from most properties.
Valet parking at Worth Avenue restaurants is the Palm Beach way — embrace it.
🚘
Private Car Service
For season travel — January through April — I recommend pre-booking a private car from the airport. Uber surge pricing at PBI during snowbird season is real and unpredictable. A flat-rate car is worth the peace of mind.
I arrange car service for my clients — one less thing to manage on arrival day.

Advisor's Palm Beach tips
  • Season runs January through April — rates and crowds peak in February and March. Book hotels and restaurants months ahead.
  • The Breakers is the full-resort experience; Four Seasons is more intimate. Both are excellent; the choice depends on your travel style.
  • Café Boulud is the best dinner reservation in town — book it the moment you confirm your trip dates.
  • Rent bikes from the hotel and ride the Lake Trail early morning — one of the most beautiful rides in Florida.
  • Pack smart-casual minimum for dinner everywhere on Worth Avenue. Palm Beach dresses for dinner.
  • Shoulder season (May, November) offers significantly lower rates with perfect weather and far less crowd.
Best times to visit
  • January – March — Peak season, perfect weather, full social calendar
  • November — Quiet, warm, and restaurants just reopening for season
  • May — Lovely weather, very low crowds, great value
  • Avoid: July–August — humid, hurricane season, many restaurants closed
