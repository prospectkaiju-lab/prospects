Where to Stay

Resorts worth every dollar

Staying on property is the single biggest advantage at Walt Disney World — early park entry, free transportation, and the ability to book dining 60 days out. Here's where I'd put my own family.

Deluxe · Top Pick
Grand Floridian Resort & Spa
Disney's flagship Victorian resort on the Magic Kingdom monorail — the closest you'll get to staying inside the fairy tale itself.
Monorail Access Victoria's & Albert's Spa Concierge Level
✦ Request a Magic Kingdom view room — watching the fireworks from your balcony is genuinely one of the most magical experiences on property.
Deluxe · Best for Families
Disney's Beach Club Resort
Stormalong Bay — the sprawling pool complex shared with the Yacht Club — is reason enough. Walking distance to Epcot is the bonus.
Walk to Epcot Stormalong Bay Pool Beaches & Cream
✦ If you're an EPCOT Food & Wine devotees, this is your resort. You can walk back for a nap mid-festival and return for dinner without a second thought.
Deluxe · Best Value Deluxe
Disney's Wilderness Lodge
National Park lodge vibes, a roaring lobby fireplace, and some of the best theming on property — at the lowest price point of any Disney deluxe resort.
Boat to Magic Kingdom Artist Point Geyser Pool
✦ The best-kept secret in Disney deluxe. The boat to Magic Kingdom feels special every single time.
Deluxe · Best Dining Views
Disney's Riviera Resort
Home to Topolino's Terrace, rooftop views of two parks' fireworks, and Skyliner access to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios. Europe-inspired and genuinely elegant.
Disney Skyliner Topolino's Terrace Rooftop Views
✦ Book Topolino's character breakfast the moment your 60-day window opens. It sells out within hours and is genuinely the best character meal on property.

Things to Do

Experiences not to miss

Walt Disney World is overwhelming by design. Here's the shortlist — experiences that are genuinely worth the lines, and the ones I'll build your entire park day around.

01
TRON Lightcycle / Run — Magic Kingdom
The fastest roller coaster at Walt Disney World, housed in a spectacular glowing structure beside Space Mountain. Book a Lightning Lane reservation the moment the park opens — or use Individual Lightning Lane at 7am.
⚡ Lightning Lane Priority
02
Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind — EPCOT
A reverse-launching indoor coaster that plays a curated 80s soundtrack synced to the ride. One of Disney's most technically impressive attractions — and the line moves slowly, so the Individual Lightning Lane is worth every dollar.
⚡ Individual Lightning Lane
03
Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge — Hollywood Studios
An entire land built around immersive storytelling. Rise of the Resistance is a must — arguably the most technically impressive attraction Disney has ever built. Smugglers Run lets you pilot the Millennium Falcon. Book both.
⚡ Lightning Lane Priority
04
EPCOT World Showcase at Night
Eleven countries, unlimited snacks and drinks, and EPCOT Forever playing over the lagoon at nightfall. The most underrated two hours at Disney World — slow down, eat your way around the world, and skip the park-hopping rush.
🌙 Evening Experience
05
Pandora — World of Avatar · Animal Kingdom
Flight of Passage is a transcendent simulator experience that still feels like the future, years after opening. The floating mountains at night are genuinely stunning. Na'vi River Journey is the calming counterpart — perfect with little ones.
⚡ Lightning Lane Priority
06
Magic Kingdom Fireworks — Happily Ever After
The nightly fireworks and projection show over Cinderella Castle. Watch from the bridge between the hub and Tomorrowland for the best sightlines, or time your California Grill reservation to watch from 15 floors up with a glass of wine.
🎆 Nightly · No Reservation
Getting There

Arriving without the stress

Orlando International Airport (MCO) is your arrival point — about 25 miles from the Walt Disney World Resort. Here's how to handle the transfer like a pro.

✈️
Flying In
Orlando International (MCO) is the primary airport, served by every major carrier. Orlando Sanford (SFB) is a secondary option but significantly farther from property and with fewer direct routes.
Book nonstops whenever possible — Orlando afternoon thunderstorms cause cascading delays in summer.
🚗
Private Car Transfer
My recommendation for families: a pre-booked private car service from MCO direct to your resort. 30–40 minutes, no waiting around, car seats arranged in advance. Runs $80–120 depending on vehicle size.
I coordinate this for my clients — one less thing to think about on arrival day.
🚌
Mears Connect
The shuttle service that replaced Magical Express. Standard (shared, multiple stops) runs ~$34/adult roundtrip. Express (direct to your resort) runs ~$250 for up to 4 guests. Reliable, but slower than private car.
Book Express, not Standard — shared shuttles can add 45+ minutes to your arrival.
🚙
Rideshare / Rental Car
Uber/Lyft runs around $40–60 from MCO (surge pricing applies). Rental cars are complimentary to park at Disney hotels. If you're planning day trips beyond property, a rental car gives you flexibility.
Schedule your Uber in advance to lock the price — surge pricing at MCO arrivals is real.
✦
On-Property Transportation
Once you're on Disney property, it's all free — buses to every park, the Disney Skyliner gondola connecting EPCOT and Hollywood Studios to select resorts, the Magic Kingdom monorail, and boats to select locations. Staying on property means you never need a car to get to a park.

Advisor's Disney tips
  • Dining reservations open exactly 60 days before check-in — set an alarm and be ready at 6am EST.
  • Lightning Lane Multi Pass is almost always worth it for families with young children — buy it the morning of, not the night before.
  • Rope drop is still the most powerful strategy at any park. Arrive 45 minutes early.
  • EPCOT's International Food & Wine Festival runs late summer through November — one of the best times to visit.
  • Deluxe resort guests get extended evening hours at select parks — a genuine perk that clears crowds dramatically.
  • Book a rest day mid-trip, especially with kids. Half-day at the pool midweek makes the whole vacation better.
Best times to visit
  • January–February — Lowest crowds of the year, comfortable weather
  • Late September–October — Mickey's Not-So-Scary, Food & Wine, mild temps
  • Early December — Christmas decor at its peak, before the holiday rush
  • Avoid: Spring Break, July 4th week, Thanksgiving, and Christmas week
